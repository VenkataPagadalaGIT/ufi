## Correction to the copy this replaces

Version 4.8, which stood in this folder from 12 October to 14 November 2026, said that the
organization operating the section studied had approved publication of the paper's figures.
It had not, and that statement is withdrawn (version 5.5, Appendix A). Version 5.5 states that
the organization was not asked to approve the paper and has not approved it, and gives the
author's reasons for not seeking approval (Section 14). The earlier files remain in this
repository's history, because the paper cites commit `149994a`.
